SWIPE2NEWS POLITICAL MEDIA LIBRARY
Research date: 16 November 2026

STATUS: Research edition. Not a fully current or cleared production image library.

Coverage: all 452 state legislative/parliamentary seat slots (119 TG MLAs, 175 AP MLAs, 40 TG council slots, 58 AP council slots, 17 TG and 25 AP Lok Sabha, 7 TG and 11 AP Rajya Sabha). Slots include vacancies/unresolved successors. National constitutional and parliamentary leaders plus the PMO Union minister list are also included. State minister/leadership roles are separate and may overlap MLA/MLC identities. Party scope is national parties plus represented/relevant TG/AP parties; not every registered Indian party.

Open source-collection.html for a searchable offline link index. The collection makes no remote image requests until you click a link. CSV/JSON contain the same data for app ingestion.

KEY GAPS
- AP NeVA member count and secondary council roster disagree. AP council member names remain provisional.
- Three Telangana nominated terms ended 14 Nov 2026. Previous holders are retained only as expired-term evidence; successor slots are unresolved.
- Current affiliation, defections and expulsions require a separate legislative recognition check. The source party label is not an independent current-party determination.
- State cabinet portfolios and national PMO list have source-date limits. AP cabinet roles are provisional and require the latest GAD order.
- Three Telangana MP portrait labels and one MLC label conflict with names. Pixel identity checks are not complete.
- Most direct portraits and party logos are missing. Each record exposes its own status.

RIGHTS
Official status does not imply public domain or a free licence. PMO permits conditional reproduction of its own material, with prominent source acknowledgement, accurate use and third-party exclusions. PIB has a separate conditional reuse policy. The President website policy requires permission. Those permissions do not automatically apply to Parliament, state portals, party logos, social posts, or press agency photos. Do not infer permission from a download button. Logo copyright and trademark/endorsement issues require a documented editorial usage decision. Fair dealing is contextual, not a blanket library licence.

No image binaries are delivered: the available download network could not retrieve them. All direct asset URLs are link records, not proof that the file remains reachable, depicts the named person, or is cleared. Credits name the publisher where the photographer is unknown; replace/add the asset-specific credit before publication.
