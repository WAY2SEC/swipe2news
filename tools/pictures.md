# Swipe2News picture menu (field "pic")

Every NEW story must carry `"pic"` – the picture that matches its MAIN EVENT (headline + picture line). Read the story, then pick ONE key below.
Ask: "Would a reader seeing only this picture guess the right topic?" If two fit, take the more specific one (n/, i/, s/, cv/ before c/).
Never pick by one word in the summary (FSSAI ≠ AI, "restore power" in a hostel protest ≠ electricity). Deaths/killings → n/condolence (never a happy picture).
District pictures (d/…) are landmarks: use them only for general local news, temples, travel or culture of that district – not for crime, health, accidents.
