AP sets maximum prices for rice varieties

The Andhra Pradesh government has fixed maximum prices for rice by variety. Common MTU rice is capped at ₹47 a kg, or ₹1,222 for a 26 kg bag. BPT and KNM varieties are ₹66 a kg, HMT and RNR ₹76, and Jai Sriram ₹82. The government warned of action against sales above these rates. Dharani rice will be sold at doorsteps through mobile vehicles.

Why it matters: Every family buying rice can compare the shop price with these maximum rates.
Source: HMTV
