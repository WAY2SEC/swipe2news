Nigeria and Togo seek investors for data parks

27.11.2026 · 06:44 WAT

Nigeria and Togo are looking for private investors to fund a joint data park project worth between $30 million and $50 million, Nairametrics, TechCabal and Peoples Gazette report. The plan is to build one facility in each country to attract international data centre operators and strengthen digital infrastructure across West Africa.
