Edo Queens open NWFL title defence on Tuesday

27.11.2026 · 05:21 WAT

Defending champions Edo Queens will begin their Nigeria Women Football League title defence against Rivers Angels on Tuesday, December 1, in the opening fixture of the 2026/27 season, the News Agency of Nigeria reports. Edo Queens go into the new campaign as the reigning league champions, with Rivers Angels their first opponents.
