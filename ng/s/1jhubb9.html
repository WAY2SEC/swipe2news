Naira gains against dollar for first time in days

25.11.2026 · 21:52 WAT

The naira strengthened to N1,392.25 per dollar on the official market on Wednesday, from N1,394.12 on Monday, Central Bank of Nigeria data cited by Daily Post show. The N1.87 gain ended a run of daily losses, a day after the currency closed at its weakest level in nearly nine months.
