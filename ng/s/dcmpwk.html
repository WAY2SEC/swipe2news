Jigawa police give N20.4m to families of late officers

28.11.2026 · 10:05 WAT

The Jigawa State Police Command has handed over N20.4 million to the relatives of officers who lost their lives while on duty, Punch reports. The money covers insurance payouts and welfare benefits owed to the families. It was presented at a ceremony held at the command in Dutse, the state capital, on Saturday.
