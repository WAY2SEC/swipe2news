Tinubu gives NAF crash families homes, N100m

26.11.2026 · 17:00 WAT

President Bola Tinubu has approved three-bedroom flats in Abuja and N100 million for the families of the 25 people killed in the Nigerian Air Force ATR 42 crash in Igbokoda, Ondo State. He announced the support on Thursday at a memorial and wreath-laying with service chiefs at the National Cemetery in Abuja, Punch reports.
