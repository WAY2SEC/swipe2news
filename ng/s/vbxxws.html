Sanwo-Olu opens 5km Ijegun-Ijagemo road

26.11.2026 · 05:44 WAT

Lagos Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu on Wednesday commissioned the 5km Ijegun/Ijagemo Road in Alimosho Local Government Area of the state, ThisDay reported. Speaking at the event, the governor said his administration had kept the promises it made to Lagos residents and would continue to deliver projects.
