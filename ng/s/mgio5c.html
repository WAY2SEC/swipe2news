Senator's aide hurt in National Assembly clash

25.11.2026 · 20:23 WAT

National Assembly management has opened an investigation after Kingsley Wali, an aide to Senator Allwell Onyesoh (Rivers East), was injured in Abuja on Wednesday. Vanguard reported he was allegedly assaulted by security operatives attached to the Senate Appropriations Committee, chaired by Senator Olamilekan Adeola, and suffered head injuries.
