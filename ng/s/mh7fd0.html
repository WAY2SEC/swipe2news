Man United draw at Sporting; Lille beat Bayern

25.11.2026 · 23:24 WAT

Bruno Fernandes scored twice in the second half but Manchester United drew 2-2 at Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League on Wednesday, so Mauricio Pochettino is still waiting for his first win as coach. A goalkeeping error by Senne Lammens let Sporting level. Elsewhere, Lille stunned Bayern Munich 2-1.
